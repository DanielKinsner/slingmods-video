# SlingMods Outro Assembly

The most templated 30 seconds in the pipeline. Measured: the final block runs **~28–30s** with striking
consistency (28.1–29.4s across measurable channel videos). Give it the full time; never rush the CTA.

## Structure
1. **Benefit recap** over the best remaining beauty/demo shot ("Once installed, you'll have…").
2. **CTA** (house formula, near-verbatim): "…head over to slingmods.com or click the link in the
   description below. And don't forget to like and subscribe…"
3. **Logo outro:** `slingmods Logo Outro 2.mov` from `03_graphics\logos\` runs to the end. Music resolves, not truncates.

## Rules
- CTA VO timing: leave the last ~8–10s VO-free over the logo sting for YouTube end-screen elements.
- The recap shot should be the product IN USE — never an install shot.
- Product name + vehicle in the CTA must match the title/thumbnail wording (consistency = search trust).
- If a promo/discount exists in the video description, it is NOT spoken in VO (channel convention: CTA
  stays evergreen; offers live in the description).

## Deliverable
Outro block on the timeline against `outro-v1.mp3`, ending flush with the logo sting's natural end.
